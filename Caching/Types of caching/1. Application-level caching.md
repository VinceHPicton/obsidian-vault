There are a few subtypes of app-level caches

**In-memory cache**
These are per instance, and could be something like a simple hashmap being used inside the app code.

Obviously they are not shared between instances so you get many more cache misses, if the instance terminates cache is lost, but they are extremely fast.



**Distributed cache**
These are caches shared across many instances, and generally use either [[Redis]] or [[Memcached]]
